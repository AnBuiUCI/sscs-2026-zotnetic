  'perfect chip' is the same reconstruction from the IDEAL readings,
  so it is what GEOMETRY AND PHYSICS put in and no circuit can remove:
  the four sensors take a finite difference over a 1 mm box, and over
  that box the field of a source 3 mm away is far from linear. It gets
  better as the source moves away. The last column, what the chip adds,
  gets WORSE as it moves away, because the signal weakens while the
  200 ppm of sensor offset stays. The two cross: there is a best
  distance, not a monotonic 'closer is better'.
